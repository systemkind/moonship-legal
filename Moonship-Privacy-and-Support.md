# Moonship: Cycle Sanctuary — Privacy Policy & Support

**Effective Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Bundle Identifier:** `app.moonship.ios`

## 1. Zero Data Collection & 100% On-Device Storage
Moonship: Cycle Sanctuary is built private by default. We do not collect, transmit, store, rent, or sell any personal data, health data, cycle logs, pain ratings, or journal entries on any external server.

- **No Accounts or Sign-In:** Moonship requires no user account, email address, or registration.
- **On-Device Encryption:** All cycle tracking records and Private Journal entries are stored locally on your iPhone using hardware-backed iOS file protection (`NSFileProtectionComplete`) and application-layer AES-GCM encryption (`CryptoKit`) keyed via the iOS Keychain.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** Moonship contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising identifiers, and zero telemetry.
- **Excluded from Automatic Cloud Backups:** By default, Moonship databases and archives are marked as excluded from automatic iCloud and iTunes backups (`isExcludedFromBackup = true`).
- **User-Initiated Sharing Only:** Data only leaves your device if you explicitly choose to send a Care Circle message via Apple Messages/Share Sheet, export a PDF/CSV summary, or export an encrypted `.moonshiparchive` backup file.

## 2. Medical Disclaimer
Moonship provides informational cycle summaries and planning estimates based solely on what you record. Moonship is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, clinical diagnosis, fertility tracking, or contraception (birth control). Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions.

## 3. Deleting Your Data
You can permanently erase all data stored by Moonship at any time inside the app via **Settings → Backup, Recovery & Reset → Delete All Data**, or by uninstalling the app from your device.
